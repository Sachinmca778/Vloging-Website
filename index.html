Government Job Age & Eligibility Calculator — How It Works

Determining your exact age on the official "Crucial Cut-Off Date" is one of the most critical preliminary steps when applying for Central and State Government recruitment notifications in India. Every major recruitment commission—including SSC, UPSC, RRB, IBPS, and State PSCs—mandates a specific cutoff date to determine candidate eligibility.

Step-by-Step Usage Guide

  1. Select Date of Birth (DOB): Choose your birth date as recorded on your 10th Class passing certificate.
  2. Input Official Crucial Date: Check the official recruitment PDF notification for the exact cut-off date.
  3. View Exact Age Calculation: The calculator outputs years, months, and days down to the exact date.

Category-Wise Upper Age Limit Relaxations (DoPT Guidelines)

  • Unreserved (UR) / EWS: Standard age limit applies (typically 18–27 or 18–30 years).
  • Other Backward Classes (OBC NCL): +3 Years upper age limit relaxation.
  • Scheduled Caste (SC) / Scheduled Tribe (ST): +5 Years upper age limit relaxation.
  • Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD): +10 Years (+13 for OBC, +15 for SC/ST).
